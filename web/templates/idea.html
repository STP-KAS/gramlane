What this is not

  • A unit of account for humans — coffee is not priced in grams.
  • A savings asset — credits are inventory of work.
  • A claim that stables are banned — liquidity will still touch USDT off to the side.
  • A clone of KNS :8080 — names live there; this lane bills the work of using them.
  • A KCC-20 named GRAM — that would be a tradable chip. The meter is a quote: KAS ↔ grams.

One line: stable work price on L1, not a synthetic dollar.
