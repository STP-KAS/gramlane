Bill the event, not the exchange rate.

You are on Gramlane. If you run a line, a substation, or a service fleet, you already budget in events: a telegram, a poll, a command. This site does the same for sequenced work on Kaspa. Grams, not dollars.

Why this is not “another DeFi app”

A control room does not want KAS/USD in the opex spreadsheet. It wants “this many sequenced jobs this month.” Work Credits are prepaid inclusion. They are closer to a message quota than to a stablecoin. KNS is the name. Kaspa Till is for when the invoice really is money.

OT already meters work

zenon (COPA-DATA) sequences process events. We do not plug into zenon. We copy the habit: a unit of work with a boring price.

Agents will sit next to SCADA

Not inside the safety loop. Next to it: reporting, quoting, calling a named service. They still need to pay for inclusion without a human clicking MetaMask.
