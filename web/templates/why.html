why · work, not rent

Bill the action, not the exchange rate.

You are on Gramlane. The scarce thing here is sequenced work: a lookup, a postage stamp, a vault bump, an agent call. Grams meter that. They do not meter rent, and they do not replace USDT.

Why this is not “another DeFi app”

A dApp that invoices in dollars has to live on someone else’s issuer. Kaspa L1 has none. So this lane invoices work. Vision is the short form. KNS is the name. Kaspa Till is for when the invoice really is money.

The fee should not move

A named job costs N grams today and N grams tomorrow. KAS/USD can thrash. That is not this meter. KAS is fallback if the voucher is empty.

Vaults still lock KAS

Grams pay the bump, not the lock. The value in the vault is still KAS. Confusing those two is how people invent fake dollars.

USDT can sit off to the side

Liquidity will touch stables. This dApp refuses to make that the protocol cost. No L2 mint, no freeze, no FX quote.
