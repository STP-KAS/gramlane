beyond the chain

Bill the event, not the exchange rate.

People do not want a new coin. They want a boring number for things they already do: a grocery, a telegram from a substation, a bot that pays a till. Put volatile KAS in one jar. Put spend in another. The spend jar is grams on Kaspa L1. It does not reprice when KAS/USD thrashes.

The household jar

You do not put the whole paycheck in the stock app. You keep some to spend. 50% aside is that habit. Grams are the jar. KAS is the rest.

The control room

A line, a poll, a command already has a unit: the event. Opex should not include a FX cell. Same as zenon metering work — we copy the habit, not the product.

The agent at the till

A machine should pay without opening a wallet tab. HTTP 402, a name, a gram bill. Human optional.

The counter

Phone facing the buyer. Same URL as the email invoice. Digital and on-location are one ticket.

What this is not

  • A dollar. Merchants who chase USD will reprice grams. Your jar still holds the same grams.
  • Tether or Circle. No mint, no freeze, no L2.
  • A KCC-20 to list on a DEX. WorkCredit is the voucher. The converter is policy arithmetic. KCC-0020 is Draft.
  • A claim that miners take grams. Miners still want KAS. You prepaid that when you filled the jar.
  • Portrait, Argent, Kassword, or a vProg exit. Those are other products. This jar is grams on L1.

One line: a jar of spend on L1, not a synthetic dollar.
