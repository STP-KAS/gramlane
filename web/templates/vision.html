Skip centralized stablecoins for dapps

project delusional A cheap L1 alternative to USDC-for-dApps. Intelligent people should look. It is still a delusional idea.

dApps invoice work in grams (KIP-21 mass), not USD. Fill is one Kaspa tx. Jobs after that do not open Kasware. Centralized stables can sit on L2. They do not sit here.

Fill is not Nakamoto for the jar

Convert is a meter. Fill is a sale to this desk. Grams live on this process’s book. Another host is another jar.

On a fillWho gets it
L1 feeMiners. That is Kaspa’s security budget. Real. Small.
L1 amountThis desk’s PayTo. Operator inventory. Not a miner reward.
GramsYour jar here. No convert-back.

Sending the fill amount to miners as extra fee would kill the cheap meter. Not worth it. The fee is the security contribution. The amount belongs to whoever hosts.

Anyone can host

Nakamoto already runs on PCs. It will not copy ledger.json. The public move is the same as a node: run the software.

  • PC: gramlane.exe · set GRAMLANE_PAYTO to your address · PUBLIC_BASE to your URL.
  • Phone: PWA of that host. No second database. Wallet signs the fill on L1.
  • Hardware: the wallet signs. The desk is still a computer.

Many hosts = many desks. Not one Circle. Also not one global gram ledger. L1 protection for grams is later consume() on WorkCredit. Until then this book is one operator. That hole is the idea. Source: STP-KAS/gramlane.
