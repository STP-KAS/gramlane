project delusional A cheap L1 alternative to USDC-for-dApps. Intelligent people should look. It is still a delusional idea.

dApps invoice work in grams (KIP-21 mass), not USD. Fill is one Kaspa tx. Jobs after that do not open Kasware. Centralised stables can sit on L2. They do not sit here.

The threat (one public host)

After this desk is on a URL, every fill is a Kaspa L1 transaction to this PayTo. Grams land on this process’s ledger.json. That is one operator book. That is a centralisation hole. Not Circle. Also not Nakamoto.

The solution that exists: anyone hosts

Nakamoto already runs on PCs (rusty-kaspa). It will not copy ledger.json. The same principle as a node: the software is public; you run it worldwide.

  • PC: gramlane.exe · PUBLIC_BASE = your URL. Fill does not pay GRAMLANE_PAYTO (that is shops).
  • Phone: PWA of that host. No second database. Wallet signs the fill on L1.
  • Hardware: the wallet signs. The desk is still a computer.

Many hosts worldwide = many desks. Not one Circle. Also not one global gram ledger. L1 protection for grams is later consume() on WorkCredit. Until then this book is one operator. That hole is the idea. How to host · source STP-KAS/gramlane.
