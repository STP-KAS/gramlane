Why not a KCC-20?

KCC-20 is a fungible token. Grams are prepaid sequenced work. Listing them on a DEX would fake a dollar. The covenant is WorkCredit (issuer, holder, credits, lane). The converter is the policy arithmetic miners already use for mass. Miners still want KAS.
