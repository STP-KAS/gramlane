What you do on Gramlane.

You buy inclusion (grams of sequenced work), not a dollar. 1 GRAM = 1 Work Credit = 1 KIP-21 gram. At the policy rate of 100 sompi/gram, 1 000 000 grams ≈ 1 KAS. USD is not quoted. Full write-up: Vision.

  2. If the browser says refused to connect, run:

3. Read the quote

  1. On the job page you see grams, prepaid remaining, a KAS fallback (sompi = grams × 100), and USD = not quoted.
  2. While remaining covers the bill, the primary button is Run — burn N grams. No second 0.5 KAS.
  3. Scheme is kaspa-work-credit. That is the gram voucher, not USDT.

3b. Desk + voucher (after the 0.5 KAS sale)

  1. Desk pay-to is kaspa:qpjm8kz…38atpq — the output of your sale tx. Not your login.
  2. That 0.5 KAS = 500 000 grams at policy. Later fills add more. /jar is the live book. /genesis is the WorkCredit instance: two keys, lane SEQ1, official silverc.
  3. The P2SH is already funded on L1. Do not deploy again. Jobs burn prepaid grams. consume() is a later 2-sig spend of that UTXO.

4. Settle — prepaid grams

  1. Open a job. Click Run — burn N grams.
  2. The sequencer subtracts N from remaining. That is operator accounting until consume(). Not a dollar. Not a new Kasware send.
  3. KAS fallback (Pay 0.5 KAS) is only when remaining cannot cover the job.
  4. WorkCredit consume is still not the job run. The voucher UTXO stays on the P2SH until both keys spend it.

5. Read the receipt

  1. You land on /run: job name, grams billed, live JSON from the public name index or api.kaspa.org.
  2. That JSON is real indexer/node data. The gram burn is not yet a chain fact. The book is /jar.
  3. Back to desk and run another job if you want.

What not to do

  • Do not treat GRAM as USD or as a live mainnet voucher you can spend in a wallet today.
  • Do not paste a seed. This site never DMs you. Safety.
  • Do not use Till (:8082) for grams — that shop is reserved kUSD, KAS settlement.
  • Do not readInputState someone else’s WorkCredit on v1-rc1. #234.
